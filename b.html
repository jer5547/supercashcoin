Steps
01

Get a Wallet

Install MetaMask (it's free and takes about 2 minutes) and add the opBNB network. This wallet is your personal key to the whole ecosystem — it's where you'll hold your $SCC and receive your USDT rewards. No sign-ups, no middleman, you stay in full control.

02

Get BNB For Gas

You need a tiny bit of BNB to cover network fees on opBNB — usually just a few cents per transaction. Buy BNB on Binance (or any exchange), withdraw it to BSC, then bridge it over to opBNB. It's a one-time setup that keeps your fees almost free from then on.

03

Buy USDT

Grab some USDT on Binance or any exchange you like, then bridge it over to opBNB. USDT is the trading currency for $SCC and the exact token your rewards get paid out in — so this is the fuel that powers your cashflow.

04

Buy Super Cash Coin

Head to the Trade Centre and swap your USDT for $SCC. Turn on auto-reinvest to automatically compound your rewards into more coins — then sit back and watch the USDT roll into your wallet on every single transaction.
